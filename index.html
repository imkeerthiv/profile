About

I care about software that works the way people expect it to, especially on a busy restaurant floor or a stockroom with patchy Wi‑Fi.

My work starts with the requirements: breaking them down, designing tests with boundary values, equivalence classes and decision tables, then running functional, exploratory and regression passes. When something is worth repeating, I automate it. I've built Selenium (Java) + TestNG frameworks for web and API, and Appium + TypeScript frameworks for iOS and Android.
